PowerSleep

About Us: The PowerSleep Mission

We are Sleepy Heads, a multi-disciplinary team of students from James Madison University. Our project, PowerSleep, was born from a simple, relatable problem: waking up is hard, and poor sleep habits are a direct drain on mental and physical health. We believe that technology should be a "force multiplier" for your well-being, not just another screen to stare at.

What is PowerSleep?

PowerSleep is an open-source home automation hub designed to turn your morning struggle into a seamless, high-performance routine. By integrating your Google Calendar, real-time Weather, and Spotify, PowerSleep automates your environment to help you rise naturally. Our centerpiece is Jarvis, an advanced AI companion capable of speech-to-text and natural voice interaction. Jarvis doesn't just display your day—he talks you through it, manages your smart home hardware like blinds and lights, and helps you stay on track with your habits.

Our Motivation

We are invested in this because we’ve lived the problem. As students, we know how easy it is to sacrifice sleep, and we wanted to build the tool we wish we had to optimize our own "sleepmaxxing" routines. Unlike rigid, closed-source smart home systems, PowerSleep is built to be consumer-supportive and innovative. We provide a premium AI experience that focuses on resiliency, using agentic capabilities to make waking up fun, productive, and—most importantly—better for your soul.

Quick Stats & Info:

The Team: A diverse group including Layla, Nate, Enzo, Ben, Jack, and Charlie.

The Tech: Powered by an agentic LLM framework, integrated with Spotify and Google APIs, and hosted for a fully interactive experience.

Our Goal: To fix sleeping habits and improve societal mental health through accessible, open-source infrastructure.
